# MY UX Portfolio

**Live Demo:** **[View Project](https://lorraine14.github.io/UX_Portfolio/)**

---

## Overview

As I learned more about UI/UX design, I wanted a place to showcase not only my final designs but also the thinking behind them. This portfolio brings together my design projects, case studies, prototypes, and wireframes in one responsive website, making it easy for recruiters and employers to explore both my design process and technical skills.

---

## The Problem

Design work is often scattered across different platforms, making it difficult for recruiters to understand the full design process behind each project.

---

## The Solution

A responsive portfolio website that organizes projects, case studies, prototypes, and wireframes into a single, easy to navigate experience.

---

## How It Works

```text
View Portfolio
      ↓
Browse Featured Projects
      ↓
Explore Case Studies
      ↓
Access Figma Prototypes & Wireframes
```
